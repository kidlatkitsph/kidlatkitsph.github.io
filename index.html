FAQ

Gumagawa ba kayo ng buong thesis?

Hindi po. We tutor and help build the hardware/prototype, and we explain every part until you can defend it yourself. We don't write chapters, papers or defence answers.

Kailan kayo available?

Weekends (Saturday–Sunday) for builds, meet-ups and full replies. Weekday messages are answered by our auto-reply and followed up in the evenings or on the weekend.

What does the 30-day warranty cover?

Faults in our own wiring or code, fixed free within 30 days of handover. Not covered: physical or water damage, wrong power supply, changes by others, or modules that fail on their own (we'll help replace them at cost).

Will the SMS kit work in my area?

K2 uses a 2G-only modem and only works where 2G is still available; treat it as a learning kit. For long-term use, choose K3 (4G LTE).

Paano ang delivery ng kits?

Free meet-up at any LRT-1 station (Fernando Poe Jr. to Dr. Santos), Lalamove same-day in Metro Manila, or J&T/LBC nationwide. Shipping is quoted before you pay.

Paano bumili ng guide?

Message us on Messenger, pay via GCash or Maya, and we send the PDF + code by Messenger or email (or a Google Drive link).

Do you issue invoices?

Yes. Registered businesses issue invoices for sales of ₱500 or more. See our registration details below.
